Each pair uses identical camera coordinates, zoom, neutral clay material, viewport and fixed white studio lights. Before is the preserved 7a6a104 sculpt; after is the reference-guided refinement. Artistic review is pending.

The 7a6a104 model is the foundation. This pass lengthens the upper skull, raises the frontal hairline, sweeps the fringe away from the brows and widens the lateral hair envelope and curl groups. Facial geometry below the upper-head transition is retained. Production, animation, rigging and audio remain unchanged.

Diagnostic colors: skin gray, lips coral, gums blue, teeth ivory, cavity purple. Surfaces remain opaque so geometry cannot be hidden by transparency. The silhouettes use an unlit black material on white.

Review limitation: some curl shapes remain tubular, and supporting hair volume is still visible at parts of the hairline and crown. This candidate is not a completed match to the supplied hair references.
