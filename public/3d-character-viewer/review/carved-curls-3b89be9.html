Each pair uses identical camera coordinates, zoom, neutral clay material, viewport and fixed white studio lights. Before is the preserved 0054631 sculpt; after is the reference-guided refinement. Artistic review is pending.

The 0054631 sculpt is preserved as the comparison. The high hairline, forehead, skull and complete face geometry are unchanged. The new groom replaces swept tubes with joined pear-shaped curl clumps, carved spiral channels and swept folds. A fuller temporal-to-occipital halo supports every clump. Wire flyaways are removed.

Diagnostic colors: skin gray, lips coral, gums blue, teeth ivory, cavity purple. Surfaces remain opaque so geometry cannot be hidden by transparency. The silhouettes use an unlit black material on white.

Geometry review: all curls are part of one closed connected volume. The channels, folded crests, rooted heels and tapered clump outlines are actual geometry, inspected with the same clay material and lighting. Artistic acceptance is based on these matched views, not the triangle count.
