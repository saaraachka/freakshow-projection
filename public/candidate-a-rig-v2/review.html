Independent smile-to-frown motion

The main slider moves from the original grin through a reduced smile, approximately level corners, a slight downturn, and a full stylized frown. Upper and lower lip bodies and the inner cavity share a broad perioral displacement rather than a vertical aperture scale. The corners have broader independent depression support, and the lower influence fades into the chin to limit jaw-outline distortion. Brow channels do not set the mouth mood.

Neutral now relaxes the mouth and covers more teeth; Angry lowers the corners and raises the upper lip; Surprised reduces the smile while opening the existing jaw channel. The sixth preset, Unhappy / creepy frown, lowers the corners, relaxes the cheeks and slightly lifts the inner brows. Original Manic laughter and the wide grin remain available. The prior three experimental targets remain disabled.

This is a stylized tooth-bearing frown, not a completely sealed human neutral. The jaw remains limited to 0–6°, and Surprised remains softly open rather than a pursed O. Strong lip pursing and full blink remain excluded.
